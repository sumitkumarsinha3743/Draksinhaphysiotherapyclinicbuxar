Our story · Our purpose

Experience rooted in care. Focused on your progress.

Dr. AK Sinha Physiotherapy Center has been serving the Buxar community since 1995. Built on a long-standing commitment to physiotherapy, the centre aims to help people move with greater confidence and work towards meaningful everyday goals.

Every person’s needs are different. Our approach begins with understanding your concern and goals, then discussing suitable physiotherapy and rehabilitation options for your situation.

Quick answers before you request an appointment.

Request Appointment ↗
How do I book an appointment?

Tap any “Book Appointment” button to open the Google Form, complete the requested details and submit. Wait for clinic confirmation before treating the request as booked.

Where is the clinic located?

The clinic is in Buxar, Bihar. Use the Google Maps directions link in the Contact section for navigation.

What should I bring for my first visit?

If available, bring relevant prescriptions, investigation reports, previous treatment notes and a list of current medicines. Do not delay urgent care to gather documents.

Will every treatment be suitable for me?

No single technique suits everyone. Treatment options should be selected after an appropriate assessment, considering your symptoms, health history and goals.
